Plants vs. Zombies for the Nintendo DS is the same addictive tower defense game you know and love, but now time with two screens and a stylus. It looks and sounds amazing, and has a thorough selection of minigames.

# Gameplay

In traditional tower defense fashion Plants vs. Zombies has the player defend their house from an oncoming wave of enemies (zombies) by using offensive weapons (planting various plants). The twist here, however, is that the zombies aren't following a long and winding path, but head straight for the front door. This allows for some unique and intuitive combos, such as putting a Torchwood in front of a Peashooter to light peas on fire for more damage. Players collect sun as it falls from the sky, or grow sunflowers to get more, to purchase additional plants. 

Gameplay stays engaging not just by adding new plant and zombie types, but also changing environments from day to night, front yard to backyard, then finally on to the roof. Each provides a unique challenge (backyard has a pool, roof is angled, sun doesn't drop at night). Further, special "conveyor belt" levels provide a unique challenge by providing continous, but unexpected, plants that require no sun. 

# Graphics

For a DS game, this really does look great. While sprites for the main gameplay have a more distinct black outline than larger images used, all the art still feels cohesive. The plants all have their own fun personality.
